      Frequently asked questions

      How the record is kept, and what makes it in.

      Who uses Talent Sheet?

      Agents and reps tracking their clients' portfolios. Brands scouting athletes for cap-table or ambassador-equity deals. Athletes and business managers benchmarking terms against peers. Journalists and researchers following the space.

      Where does the data come from?

      Public deal announcements, regulatory filings, the talent's own posts and sports-business press. Every deal carries a status and a source. Verified means a primary source confirms it. Reported means a credible secondary source does, and we link it.

      How up to date is it?

      Deals are researched and added as they're announced, usually within days of public reporting. Anything added in the last seven days carries a New marker.

      What counts as an investment?

      Venture and equity investments, franchise ownership stakes, ambassador deals that include equity, executive stakes, advisory roles and funds the talent founded or runs. If a public figure holds a real piece of the company, it counts.

      Do you only cover certain sports?

      No. Traditional and action sports, coaches, entertainers and executives, across any industry. The boundary is the relationship, not the sport.
