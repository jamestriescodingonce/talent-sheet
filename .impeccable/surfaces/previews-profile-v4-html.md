Scope: talent profile page (Kevin Durant as the reference record). Visitor mode: Operate/Read. Audience: agents, brands, athletes' managers, journalists checking who holds what. Job: see every holding, its relationship, its verification and its source, fast.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A profile is a share register, not a dashboard. Each holding is drawn as an engraved certificate stub. Refuses the category default of sidebar + photo banner + identical logo-card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Night ground #0A110D, cream ink #EDEBE0, one cool green (#3DDC97) used as engraving ink: guilloche rosettes, fine double-rule certificate borders, circular verification seals with lettering on the ring. Portraits in an oval vignette, printed in monotone green like banknote engraving. Bodoni Moda display, Schibsted Grotesk for everything read. No shadows, no glow.
STORY: The visitor sees who the person is, how many holdings are on record and how each is known; opens any holding to see the round and the source.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim top bar (wordmark, index, search). A full-width certificate frame: oval portrait left, name set large in Bodoni, league line, associated company, social icons, a one-line register summary; generative guilloche rosette drawn on canvas bleeding off the right edge.
